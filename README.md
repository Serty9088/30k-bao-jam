# Pentacore Events Showcase

Welcome to the **Pentacore Lucky Events** - a collection of 19 unique events created for the *Bedrock Add-Ons Server* 30k Members collaborative jam.

---

> [!NOTE]  
> You can Download **Pentacore Lucky Events.zip** file alone to avoid downloading the media folder that is used for event videos previews.

## Event Collection

### 1. `amongUsTrapRemix.js`
- **Description**: Plays the Among Us trap remix sound and calls the player "SUSSY BAKA".
- **Video Preview**:  
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Serty9088/30k-bao-jam/main//media/videos/amongUsTrapRemix.mp4
- **What Happens**: Sends a chat message calling the player "SUSSY BAKA" and plays the sound to the player.

---

### 2. `badApple.js`
- **Description**: Forces player into spectator mode to watch the Bad Apple animation.
- **Video Preview**:  
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Serty9088/30k-bao-jam/main//media/videos/badApple.mp4
- **What Happens**: Puts the player in spectator mode, disables movement and HUD, and plays the animation using spawned entities. The player can exit after a short delay.

---

### 3. `enchantedEgg.js`
- **Description**: Spawns an enchanted egg that creates chickens on impact.
- **Video Preview**:  
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Serty9088/30k-bao-jam/main//media/videos/enchantedEgg.mp4
- **What Happens**: Spawns a special egg item. When thrown, it spawns multiple chickens on impact.

---

### 4. `endlessZombie.js`
- **Description**: Spawns a fire-resistant zombie that creates sub-zombies when attacking.
- **Video Preview**:  
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Serty9088/30k-bao-jam/main//media/videos/endlessZombie.mp4
- **What Happens**: Spawns a zombie that resists fire. When it attacks, it spawns additional zombies until its counter runs out.

---

### 5. `foodPack.js`
- **Description**: Drops food items and converts the trigger block to cake.
- **Video Preview**:  
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Serty9088/30k-bao-jam/main//media/videos/foodPack.mp4
- **What Happens**: Spawns random food items around the location. After a short delay, the trigger block becomes a cake.

---

### 6. `itemsFountain.js`
- **Description**: Launches random items upward over time.
- **Video Preview**:  
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Serty9088/30k-bao-jam/main//media/videos/itemsFountain.mp4
- **What Happens**: Spawns random items one per tick and applies upward impulse so they rise and fall around the area.

---

### 7. `lord_placeholder.js`
- **Description**: Places a gold/piglin-head structure that can summon a special entity.
- **Video Preview**:  
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Serty9088/30k-bao-jam/main//media/videos/lord_placeholder.mp4
- **What Happens**: Places a predefined structure. If the correct pattern is built, it summons a custom entity.

---

### 8. `meteorRain.js`
- **Description**: Starts a meteor shower targeting the player.
- **Video Preview**:  
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Serty9088/30k-bao-jam/main//media/videos/meteorRain.mp4
- **What Happens**: Periodically spawns meteors above the player and launches them toward their position for a set duration.

---

### 9. `midasTouch.js`
- **Description**: Grants temporary ability to turn blocks and entities into gold.
- **Video Preview**:  
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Serty9088/30k-bao-jam/main//media/videos/midasTouch.mp4
- **What Happens**: For a short time, blocks under the player turn into gold blocks. Entities and blocks hit by the player are also replaced with gold blocks.

---

### 10. `minesweeper.js`
- **Description**: Teleports the player to a playable Minesweeper game.
- **Video Preview**:  
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Serty9088/30k-bao-jam/main//media/videos/minesweeper.mp4
- **What Happens**: Generates a Minesweeper board and teleports the player there. The player can reveal cells or place flags. Progress is saved.

---

### 11. `musicEvent.js`
- **Description**: Converts the trigger block to a jukebox and plays music.
- **Video Preview**:  
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Serty9088/30k-bao-jam/main//media/videos/musicEvent.mp4
- **What Happens**: Replaces the trigger block with a jukebox, inserts a random music disc, and starts playback.

---

https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Serty9088/30k-bao-jam/main//media/videos/reversedWorld.mp4

### 17. `superEquipment.js`

### 18. `tamedWolfs.js`
- **Description**: Spawns tamed wolves around the player.
- **Video Preview**:  
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Serty9088/30k-bao-jam/main//media/videos/tamedWolfs.mp4

### 19. `theFloorIsMagma.js`
- **Description**: Converts nearby blocks to magma.
- **Video Preview**:  
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Serty9088/30k-bao-jam/main//media/videos/theFloorIsMagma.mp4

## Overview

Each event activates a scripted behavior that affects the player or surrounding world in a different way.
