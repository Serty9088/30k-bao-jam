# Pentacore Events Showcase

Welcome to the **Pentacore Lucky Events** - a collection of 19 unique events created for the *Bedrock Add-Ons Server* 30k Members collaborative jam.

---

> [!NOTE]  
> You can download **Pentacore Lucky Events.zip** file alone.

## Event Collection

### 1. `amongUsTrapRemix.js`
- **Description**: Plays the Among Us trap remix sound and calls the player "SUSSY BAKA".
- **Video Preview**:

https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/0fa5b013-341c-4a4c-8a4b-eb5f8e2ef1b0

- **What Happens**: Sends a chat message calling the player "SUSSY BAKA" and plays the sound to the player.

---

### 2. `badApple.js`
- **Description**: Forces player into spectator mode to watch the Bad Apple animation.
- **Video Preview**:  

https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/f5c4331c-967e-4e22-b610-2ab245d487db

- **What Happens**: Puts the player in spectator mode, disables movement and HUD, and plays the animation using spawned entities. The player can exit after a short delay.

---

### 3. `enchantedEgg.js`
- **Description**: Spawns an enchanted egg that creates chickens on impact.
- **Video Preview**:  

https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/a5ba89a8-ca7a-4478-8736-7552b3c394f6

- **What Happens**: Spawns a special egg item. When thrown, it spawns multiple chickens on impact.

---

### 4. `endlessZombie.js`
- **Description**: Spawns a fire-resistant zombie that creates sub-zombies when attacking.
- **Video Preview**:  

https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/4d8278ba-2b8a-4c5e-b624-824c26ddcd95

- **What Happens**: Spawns a zombie that resists fire. When it attacks, it spawns additional zombies until its counter runs out.

---

### 5. `foodPack.js`
- **Description**: Drops food items and converts the trigger block to cake.
- **Video Preview**:  

https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/4d0ec689-f1f1-4830-85cd-3cdd24349202

- **What Happens**: Spawns random food items around the location. After a short delay, the trigger block becomes a cake.

---

### 6. `itemsFountain.js`
- **Description**: Launches random items upward over time.
- **Video Preview**:

https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/dacee607-15fd-42bf-974b-bb61f24f4827

- **What Happens**: Spawns random items one per tick and applies upward impulse so they rise and fall around the area.

---

### 7. `lord_placeholder.js`
- **Description**: Places a gold/piglin-head structure that can summon a special entity.
- **Video Preview**:  

https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/9e1214e4-85b1-4eb8-85bb-89a73fe2aad1

- **What Happens**: Places a predefined structure. If the correct pattern is built, it summons a custom entity.

---

### 8. `meteorRain.js`
- **Description**: Starts a meteor shower targeting the player.
- **Video Preview**:  

https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/b05a15b3-f7c1-458f-b6be-4d1eafd90fe0

- **What Happens**: Periodically spawns meteors above the player and launches them toward their position for a set duration.

---

### 9. `midasTouch.js`
- **Description**: Grants temporary ability to turn blocks and entities into gold.
- **Video Preview**:  

https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/c8aedad8-d4b8-47f1-897b-f8f0931b97a8

- **What Happens**: For a short time, blocks under the player turn into gold blocks. Entities and blocks hit by the player are also replaced with gold blocks.

---

### 10. `minesweeper.js`

- **Video Preview**:  

https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/d6e72c2a-cd0f-4f7c-b8d8-41778e8c7637
